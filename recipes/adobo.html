Chicken Adobo

About Chicken Adobo

Chicken Adobo is one of the most popular dishes in the Philippines. It is prepared by marinating chicken in soy sauce and garlic, then cooking it with vinegar, bay leaves, and peppercorns. The result is tender chicken with a rich sauce that goes well with steamed white rice.

Recipe Information

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Marinating Time: At least 1 hour
  • Cooking Time: 50 minutes
  • Servings: 4 people
  • Cuisine: Filipino

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 4 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 10 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • Salt to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Prepare the chicken by cutting it into serving-sized pieces. Crush the garlic cloves to release their flavor.
  2. Place the chicken in a large bowl. Add the soy sauce and crushed garlic. Mix until the chicken is coated evenly.
  3. Let the chicken marinate for at least 1 hour. You may marinate it longer in the refrigerator for more flavor.
  4. Remove the chicken from the marinade. Keep the remaining marinade and garlic because they will be used later.
  5. Heat 3 tablespoons of cooking oil in a pot over medium heat. Once the oil is hot, fry the chicken for about 2 minutes on each side.
  6. Take the chicken out of the pot. Add the remaining tablespoon of oil, followed by the garlic from the marinade. Saute until lightly browned and fragrant.
  7. Return the chicken to the pot. Add the reserved marinade, dried bay leaves, and whole peppercorns.
  8. Pour in 1 1/2 cups of water and bring the mixture to a boil. Cover the pot, lower the heat, and simmer for around 30 minutes or until the chicken is tender.
  9. Add the white vinegar. Allow it to boil for about 1 minute before stirring. Continue cooking without the lid for another 15 minutes.
  10. Add the sugar and adjust the seasoning with salt if needed. Stir gently and turn off the heat.
  11. Transfer the chicken to a serving dish. Pour some sauce over it and serve with freshly cooked rice.

Cooking Tips

  • Chicken thighs and drumsticks are good choices because they remain tender.
  • Allow the vinegar to boil before stirring to reduce its sharp raw taste.
  • Marinating the chicken longer can help develop a stronger flavor.
  • You can simmer the sauce a little longer if you prefer a thicker consistency.
