Chicken Adobo

A delicious Filipino chicken dish.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Soy sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Garlic

Instructions

  1. Prepare the ingredients.
  2. Marinate the chicken.
  3. Cook the chicken with the sauce.
  4. Simmer until fully cooked.
  5. Serve with rice.
